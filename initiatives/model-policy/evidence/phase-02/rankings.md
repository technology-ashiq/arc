## Reading

The two rankings **disagree**, and both **alternate perfectly**. Neither arm dominates on
either reading — the interleaving assumption A-01 named as its own death condition. A-01 is
dead: the workhorse composer seat is not a demonstrated quality bottleneck on this brief.

That the owner and the jury invert each other is itself the finding. A single agent juror is
not a substitute for the owner's eyes, and this run is a concrete instance of the two
disagreeing on the same six artifacts under the same brief.
