What the rejected attempt taught
--------------------------------
decision.recorded cannot be emitted with `arc-event.sh emit`. Beyond the closed payload
shape (decides / verdict / reason), the emitter enforces:

    decision.idem must be sha256("decision.recorded|" + decides)

The idem is WELDED to the approval it decides, and the raw emitter does not compute it.
`arc-inbox approve <id> --reason ...` does. The phase-04 spec said to use the raw emitter
and was wrong; it has been corrected in the same commit as this file.
