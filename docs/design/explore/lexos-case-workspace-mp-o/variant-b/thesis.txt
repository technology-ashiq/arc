VARIANT B — narrative (content-led, paced)
==========================================

THESIS
This product wins because the user can read the case's whole life as one dated record, with what
is still owed standing above today's line and what happened below it, without ever choosing a
category for the thing he is looking for or for the thing he is about to write down.

ART DIRECTION
Its art direction is a printed case diary: a warm parchment ground with sepia-black ink and sepia
hairline rules, one structural hue (ink blue, spent on dates and on today's rule — here colour
marks time, never status) plus an oxblood that appears exactly once on the page, on the
destructive verb; one old-style serif worked hard across seven live steps, with real italics
carrying entry type and small caps carrying labels; tight inside an entry and very generous
between periods; and a surface that is type and rules only — it refuses cards, chips, pills,
shadows, iconography of every kind, and sans-serif anywhere in the content.


WHAT THE PAGE IS BUILT OUT OF — the structural contract for B
-------------------------------------------------------------

1. PRIMARY OBJECT — the dated entry, in one continuous record.
   Its type (hearing · document · task · note) is a label carried in the margin, never a container
   it lives inside. There are no sections on this page. What the case still owes is an entry that
   has not happened yet, sitting above today's rule.

2. PRIMARY ACTION — write the next dated fact at today's rule.
   ONE compose affordance, sitting on the Today line, where the new entry will land. Recording
   what happened and adding what is due next are the SAME act, because in a record they are one
   dated fact and its consequence. The entry's type is chosen INSIDE that one act, never before
   it. No per-section verbs, no actions panel, no second compose point.

3. INFO BEFORE ACTION — a standing cover sheet.
   The always-visible quartet sits above the record's first entry, the way a case file's cover
   sheet precedes its pages. The next hearing is stated TWICE: once in the cover sheet, and once
   above today's rule as the entry that has not happened ("Next hearing — Not set").

4. NAVIGATION MODEL — chronology is the address.
   Sticky dated period heads are the only navigation control; they collapse and expand in place.
   Today's rule is the origin of the page. No section list, no type tab, no step, no drill-in.

5. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE — temporal, by period.
   Everything above today's rule and the current period are open in full; older periods collapse
   to one dated summary line ("Mar–Jun 2026 · 1 hearing · 2 documents · 1 note") that expands in
   place. The future is exactly the dated obligations that exist — nothing speculative, no
   placeholder for a hearing nobody has set.

6. EXPERT PATH — keys over dates and entries.
   Jump to today's rule; jump to the pending set above it; a "since you last opened" marker so
   only new entries are read; a type lens that narrows WITHIN the stream without ever leaving it.
   Expertise here is reading less of a record you already know.

7. FAILURE / RECOVERY — the failed entry stays in the stream at its date.
   It is flagged not recorded, with what failed and the next step stated in the entry itself. The
   record cannot read as current while an entry is unrecorded. Return opens at today's rule, not
   at the top of the file.

DEBT 1 (the shipped content column is frozen at 416px from 1024px upward) — B's answer:
   REPLACE THE MEASURE WITH A READING MEASURE. Still one column, but sized for reading — about
   62–68 characters, comfortably wider than 416px — with a left marginalia gutter for dates and
   entry type. Width buys line length and margin, nothing else. If B ever opens a second content
   region it has stopped being B.

DEBT 2 (the shipped 16rem actions panel offers all four actions on every tab) — B's answer:
   NO PANEL; ONE COMPOSE POINT. A single affordance on today's rule. Nothing else on the page
   initiates anything.

MOBILE (required):
   Natively one column. Period heads stay sticky; marginalia moves above its entry; the compose
   point stays on today's rule.


ART DIRECTION IN FULL — the four axes, all four are contract
------------------------------------------------------------

PALETTE — warm paper, sepia ink.
   Ground parchment (~#f4efe6). Ink a warm sepia-black (~#241f1a). Rules hairline sepia
   (~#cbbba4). ONE STRUCTURAL HUE: ink blue (~#1f3a6e), spent on dates and on today's rule —
   COLOUR MARKS TIME HERE, NEVER STATUS. Overdue is carried by the word and by weight, not by a
   colour. One reserved hue, oxblood (~#7b2230), appearing EXACTLY ONCE on the page, on the
   destructive verb. Hexes are direction, not specification: hit the hue and the temperature,
   tune the value until it passes contrast.
   REFUSES: pure white, pure black, status fills, coloured chips, and any hue used to mean a
   state.

TYPOGRAPHY — one old-style serif, worked hard.
   Real italics carrying entry type. Small caps carrying labels. Body at genuine reading size
   with generous leading. Old-style figures in prose against LINING TABULAR figures in the date
   margin, so dates align down the column. Dated period heads big enough to pace the page
   (2.5–3rem). SEVEN live steps — this is the widest scale in the explore and it must be visibly
   wide. The first family in the stack must be a genuinely available old-style or transitional
   serif with no network (Constantia / Cambria / Palatino Linotype / Georgia) — a webfont that
   fails to fetch destroys this direction.
   REFUSES: sans-serif anywhere in the content, all-caps headlines, condensed faces, and bold as
   the main emphasis — italics and small caps do that work.

DENSITY & RHYTHM — unhurried, read down, slowly.
   One reading column with a marginalia gutter. Tight inside an entry; VERY generous between
   periods — the air between periods is the instrument and is the largest interval on the page.
   Dated period heads set the pace and nothing else does.

SURFACE & ORNAMENT — a printed page.
   No cards, no shadow, no rounded corners, no fills. A sepia hairline rule system. A running
   head that behaves like a printed folio. LEFT-MARGIN MARGINALIA carrying date and entry type
   instead of chips. One typographic ornament between periods.
   REFUSES: boxes, pills, chips, and iconography of every kind — this record is type and rules
   only.

WHAT THIS VARIANT MUST NOT BECOME
   This thesis is one continuous record, read down. If the page ever splits into parallel
   columns or ledgers, or grows a rail of destinations, or turns its periods into numbered stages
   with a next button and a finished screen, or puts a second affordance anywhere other than
   today's rule — it has stopped being this thesis, however good it looks.

CANONICAL COPY FOR THE STATES — use these words, do not write your own
  loading      The case header and your page's primary region each need a designed loading state.
               No copy: quiet placeholder shapes in your own visual system. A region that shows
               nothing while it waits is not a loading state, and the shipped build's absence of
               one is a named defect this explore must answer.
  empty        "Next hearing — Not set" on this case, and Latha Bhaskaran's empty case-number
               field, are the real empty states. An empty field still shows: in a legal record an
               empty field is information.
  error        "Not saved — the connection dropped. What you typed is kept here. Save again."
  success      The recorded fact appears in place, dated — "Outcome recorded · 14 Jul 2026" — and
               what is due next recomputes in the same view. Recording an outcome does NOT change
               the overdue count: it stays 2, because neither the task nor the filing was
               touched. Do not invent different arithmetic.
  disabled     A blocked affordance states its reason: "Cannot add the next hearing until the
               14 Jul 2026 outcome is recorded."
  destructive  "Remove the document Affidavit of the plaintiff? A removed document cannot be
               brought back." A destructive verb is never styled like the benign verb beside it.
  end state    Only if your thesis renders a finished screen, verbatim: "Nothing due on this case
               today. Next: Vishnu Menon v. Ashirvad Developers · hearing 04 Aug 2026."

--- SHARED FLOOR — IDENTICAL IN A, B AND C. Not a divergence surface. ---

A variant that "innovates" on anything in this block has diverged on the wrong axis.

- ALWAYS VISIBLE, at all times, in every variant: parties · case number (where one exists) ·
  status · next hearing · overdue count. Never behind a click, a period, a step, a keystroke or a
  scroll.
- A FIELD THAT EXISTS AND IS EMPTY STILL SHOWS. "Next hearing — Not set" is information in a
  legal record. Never hidden, never collapsed, never a bare dash.
- ALL FIVE STATES ARE REAL STATES — empty · loading · error · success · disabled — rendered where
  they naturally occur. Keep the state matrix OFF the product page. If you need a reference
  block, put it at the foot, mark it as reference, and keep it visually subordinate. A previous
  cycle let state galleries eat 40–60% of every variant's page; that alone reads as BELOW-BAR.
- CLOSED VOCABULARY. Nouns: case · hearing · document · task · note · client · firm · case number
  · court · case type · filing. Verbs: add · edit · remove · save · record · file. `set`,
  `schedule`, `reschedule`, `close`, `reopen`, `approve`, `complete` are NOT verbs in this
  product — "set the next hearing" renders as "Add hearing". "Hearing outcome" is sanctioned.
  A document's own TITLE is content a lawyer typed and may be real court vocabulary; an interface
  LABEL — button, column head, step name, status, column of a table — may use only the closed set.
- STATUSES ARE EXACTLY INTAKE · ACTIVE · ON_HOLD · CLOSED, rendered as human text ("On hold",
  never ON_HOLD), and never carried by colour alone. No fifth status, and no status-shaped word
  invented for a step, a period, a row state or a queue.
- VOICE: a court record, not an assistant. State the fact and its date. No exclamation marks, no
  encouragement, no congratulation for filing something. Anti-words: urgent · flimsy · chatty.
  Feel words: exact · unhurried · durable.
- DESTRUCTIVE LANGUAGE names the thing and states that it cannot be undone; errors say what
  failed and what to do next.
- DENSITY RULES: one row per hearing, per document, per task · dates and amounts in tabular
  numerals so they align down a column · a row's meta separated by " · " consistently.
- DATES as 27 Jul 2026. ₹ AMOUNTS in Indian digit grouping (₹18,45,000). Lorem ipsum is an
  automatic VIOLATION anywhere, including inside a collapsed period, a truncated ledger or a
  disabled step.
- NEITHER SHIPPED SHAPE SURVIVES: no variant keeps a vertical 16rem rail of buttons, and no
  variant keeps a five-item section tab rail. A page with either has re-skinned the baseline
  instead of answering the two debts this explore exists to answer.
- A11Y FLOOR: >=4.5:1 on every text pair you rely on, measured against YOUR OWN declared values
  and commented in tokens.css · targets >=44px · visible focus · reduced motion honoured. Colour
  is never the only carrier of a state: every coloured thing also carries a word or a mark.
- DESKTOP AND MOBILE are both required; tablet is declared `no`. State your reflow in the page —
  a layout that only works at 1280px is not a thesis.
