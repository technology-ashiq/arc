VARIANT C — guided workflow (steps, progressive disclosure)
==========================================================

THESIS
This product wins because the user can finish this case for the day in a single decision and see
on sight that it is finished, without holding in his head what else the case owes or deciding
which of four buttons applies.

ART DIRECTION
Its art direction is one decision on a quiet sheet: a near-white porcelain ground carrying a
single solid deep-court-green block that IS the live step and moves as the run advances, burnt
clay reserved for what is overdue, a neo-grotesk sans in four steps with a violent jump between
the step statement (48px+) and everything else, whole-line-height air around the live block with
everything not live compressed to one line, and a strictly flat surface — it refuses shadows and
elevation, hairline table grids, icon sets, serifs, and more than two lines of body copy
anywhere.


WHAT THE PAGE IS BUILT OUT OF — the structural contract for C
-------------------------------------------------------------

1. PRIMARY OBJECT — the step: the case's current position in an ordered run.
   The case's sections are the steps' CONTENTS, not destinations. The run has a first step, a
   current step, and a declared end.

2. PRIMARY ACTION — fire the current step's single verb.
   `Record hearing outcome` now; `Add hearing` only after that; `File document` only inside its
   own step. EXACTLY ONE primary button is on screen at any time, and it changes as the run
   advances. The other verbs are not secondary buttons — they live inside steps that are not
   open, and a blocked step states its reason instead of offering a button.

3. INFO BEFORE ACTION — the quartet, plus this step's precondition and nothing else.
   The always-visible quartet as a fixed header line, then the current step's precondition:
   "Hearing held 14 Jul 2026 · outcome not recorded". That is enough to act; the history is not
   needed to take the step and is not shown.

4. NAVIGATION MODEL — an ordered run with a declared end state.
   A HORIZONTAL step rail, never a vertical panel. Steps are addressable and revisitable but
   ordered. Completed steps collapse to a one-line dated done state. The run terminates in a REAL
   SCREEN — the fixture's end sentence rendered as a state of the page, not as a label on a chip.

5. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE — per step.
   Only the current step is open. Each step shows only its own slice: the documents step shows
   the document THAT STEP is about, not the document list. The whole record is exactly ONE
   collapsed disclosure at the foot of the run — one, not two, and it does not open by default.

6. EXPERT PATH — keys over steps.
   One key fires the current step's single verb; one key walks the run; at the end state the same
   key opens the next case whose run is not finished. Expertise here is never having to choose
   which action to take.

7. FAILURE / RECOVERY — the run stalls at the step.
   The step stays open with what failed and the next step stated in it, and later steps stay
   explicitly disabled with their stated reason ("Cannot add the next hearing until the
   14 Jul 2026 outcome is recorded"). Return opens at the stalled or current step, never at a
   section.

DEBT 1 (the shipped content column is frozen at 416px from 1024px upward) — C's answer:
   SPEND WIDTH ON SCALE, NOT ON COLUMNS. One decision region; no measure on the step statement
   (it is display type); body text capped at two lines. Width buys size and air around the one
   live thing.

DEBT 2 (the shipped 16rem actions panel offers all four actions on every tab) — C's answer:
   NO PANEL; ONE BUTTON, EVER. Exactly one primary button on screen at a time — the current
   step's single verb.

MOBILE (required):
   One step per screen, which is this run's natural shape. The horizontal rail compresses to
   "Step 1 of 3" plus the step's own name. The green block goes full-bleed minus a margin.

THE RUN, FIXED AT ASSIGNMENT TIME:
   Three ordered steps, named ONLY with closed verbs —
   `Record hearing outcome` -> `Add hearing` -> `File document`.
   No step may be invented for an obligation the closed verb set cannot name: there is no verb
   for completing a task and none is to be introduced. The two overdue items still appear in the
   header's overdue count and in the collapsed record at the foot; they do not become steps.


ART DIRECTION IN FULL — the four axes, all four are contract
------------------------------------------------------------

PALETTE — a quiet sheet with one solid block.
   Ground near-white porcelain (~#fbfbfa) with near-black text. ONE MEANING HUE, USED AS A LARGE
   SOLID FIELD: deep court green (~#0f3d2e) — the live step is a green block with bone type
   inside it, and the block MOVES down the run as steps complete. Burnt clay (~#9a3b1e) is
   reserved for what is overdue and appears nowhere else. Hexes are direction, not specification:
   hit the hue and the temperature, tune the value until it passes contrast.
   REFUSES: dark page grounds, tinted panels everywhere, a third hue, and colour used at hairline
   sizes — this palette works in whole blocks or it does not work.

TYPOGRAPHY — a neo-grotesk sans at display scale.
   The step statement is set at 48px+ and is the loudest thing on the page. Labels are small,
   wide-tracked caps. Dates are oversized tabular numerals. FOUR steps and nothing in the middle
   — the rhythm is the gap between the huge and the small, not a gentle ramp. The first family in
   the stack must be genuinely available with no network — a webfont that fails to fetch destroys
   this direction.
   REFUSES: serifs, italics, more than two lines of body copy anywhere, and type used to fill
   space.

DENSITY & RHYTHM — one decision per viewport.
   Space measured in whole line-heights around the live block; everything not live compressed to
   a single line. The page is mostly empty on purpose, and that emptiness is what makes
   "finished" legible at a glance.

SURFACE & ORNAMENT — solid blocks, strictly flat.
   The live step is a solid green block; done steps are one flat line each; blocked steps are
   flat with their reason in text. ELEVATION IS REFUSED OUTRIGHT — state is carried by block
   versus line, and by colour plus word, never by depth. One heavy step numeral. One rule running
   the length of the run.
   REFUSES: shadows, elevation, hairline table grids, icon sets, centred layout.

WHAT THIS VARIANT MUST NOT BECOME
   This thesis is one thing at a time, ordered, ending. If the page ever opens every step at once
   with its own button, or spreads the case across parallel ledgers, or turns the foot disclosure
   into a full dated stream that is open by default, or ends without a real finished screen — it
   has stopped being this thesis, however good it looks.

CANONICAL COPY FOR THE STATES — use these words, do not write your own
  loading      The case header and your page's primary region each need a designed loading state.
               No copy: quiet placeholder shapes in your own visual system. A region that shows
               nothing while it waits is not a loading state, and the shipped build's absence of
               one is a named defect this explore must answer.
  empty        "Next hearing — Not set" on this case, and Latha Bhaskaran's empty case-number
               field, are the real empty states. An empty field still shows: in a legal record an
               empty field is information.
  error        "Not saved — the connection dropped. What you typed is kept here. Save again."
  success      The recorded fact appears in place, dated — "Outcome recorded · 14 Jul 2026" — and
               what is due next recomputes in the same view. Recording an outcome does NOT change
               the overdue count: it stays 2, because neither the task nor the filing was
               touched. Do not invent different arithmetic.
  disabled     A blocked affordance states its reason: "Cannot add the next hearing until the
               14 Jul 2026 outcome is recorded."
  destructive  "Remove the document Affidavit of the plaintiff? A removed document cannot be
               brought back." A destructive verb is never styled like the benign verb beside it.
  end state    Only if your thesis renders a finished screen, verbatim: "Nothing due on this case
               today. Next: Vishnu Menon v. Ashirvad Developers · hearing 04 Aug 2026."

--- SHARED FLOOR — IDENTICAL IN A, B AND C. Not a divergence surface. ---

A variant that "innovates" on anything in this block has diverged on the wrong axis.

- ALWAYS VISIBLE, at all times, in every variant: parties · case number (where one exists) ·
  status · next hearing · overdue count. Never behind a click, a period, a step, a keystroke or a
  scroll.
- A FIELD THAT EXISTS AND IS EMPTY STILL SHOWS. "Next hearing — Not set" is information in a
  legal record. Never hidden, never collapsed, never a bare dash.
- ALL FIVE STATES ARE REAL STATES — empty · loading · error · success · disabled — rendered where
  they naturally occur. Keep the state matrix OFF the product page. If you need a reference
  block, put it at the foot, mark it as reference, and keep it visually subordinate. A previous
  cycle let state galleries eat 40–60% of every variant's page; that alone reads as BELOW-BAR.
- CLOSED VOCABULARY. Nouns: case · hearing · document · task · note · client · firm · case number
  · court · case type · filing. Verbs: add · edit · remove · save · record · file. `set`,
  `schedule`, `reschedule`, `close`, `reopen`, `approve`, `complete` are NOT verbs in this
  product — "set the next hearing" renders as "Add hearing". "Hearing outcome" is sanctioned.
  A document's own TITLE is content a lawyer typed and may be real court vocabulary; an interface
  LABEL — button, column head, step name, status, column of a table — may use only the closed set.
- STATUSES ARE EXACTLY INTAKE · ACTIVE · ON_HOLD · CLOSED, rendered as human text ("On hold",
  never ON_HOLD), and never carried by colour alone. No fifth status, and no status-shaped word
  invented for a step, a period, a row state or a queue.
- VOICE: a court record, not an assistant. State the fact and its date. No exclamation marks, no
  encouragement, no congratulation for filing something. Anti-words: urgent · flimsy · chatty.
  Feel words: exact · unhurried · durable.
- DESTRUCTIVE LANGUAGE names the thing and states that it cannot be undone; errors say what
  failed and what to do next.
- DENSITY RULES: one row per hearing, per document, per task · dates and amounts in tabular
  numerals so they align down a column · a row's meta separated by " · " consistently.
- DATES as 27 Jul 2026. ₹ AMOUNTS in Indian digit grouping (₹18,45,000). Lorem ipsum is an
  automatic VIOLATION anywhere, including inside a collapsed period, a truncated ledger or a
  disabled step.
- NEITHER SHIPPED SHAPE SURVIVES: no variant keeps a vertical 16rem rail of buttons, and no
  variant keeps a five-item section tab rail. A page with either has re-skinned the baseline
  instead of answering the two debts this explore exists to answer.
- A11Y FLOOR: >=4.5:1 on every text pair you rely on, measured against YOUR OWN declared values
  and commented in tokens.css · targets >=44px · visible focus · reduced motion honoured. Colour
  is never the only carrier of a state: every coloured thing also carries a word or a mark.
- DESKTOP AND MOBILE are both required; tablet is declared `no`. State your reflow in the page —
  a layout that only works at 1280px is not a thesis.

--- END SHARED FLOOR ---
