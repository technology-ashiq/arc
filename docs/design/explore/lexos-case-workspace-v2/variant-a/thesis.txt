This product wins because the user can see every obligation this case carries set row-for-row against what is already on the record, and close any one of them where it stands, without opening a section, reading the history, or being told which one to do first.

Art direction: a cold audit desk — a pale blue-grey ground that is never white, carrying exactly two meaning-bearing hues, deep petrol for what is on record and vermilion for what is not, each always paired with a word so colour is never the only carrier; two typefaces on one page on purpose, a tight mechanical grotesk in caps and tabular figures down the required side against a transitional legal serif down the record side; dense ruled columns on a strict grid where the only air on the page is the gutter the eye reads across; flat hairline-ruled surfaces with dotted leader rules tying each requirement to its match or to the void where the match should be — it refuses white grounds, warm neutrals, cards, shadows, illustration, icon sets and any third accent colour.
