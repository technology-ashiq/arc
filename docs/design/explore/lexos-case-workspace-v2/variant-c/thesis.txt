This product wins because the user can finish a case for the day in a single decision and know on sight that it is finished, without holding in his head what else the case might owe or when it will next need him.

Art direction: a lit desk — a deep ink-navy field with one bone sheet raised on it and a single high-chroma marigold that touches nothing but the live action and the overdue count, every other tone muted so the bright thing is unmistakable; one humanist geometric sans worked at a violent scale jump, a step statement above 40px against small wide-tracked caps labels and oversized tabular dates, with nothing in between; one decision per viewport, air measured in whole line-heights, every step that is not live compressed to a single line; and genuine elevation used as meaning — the live step is a shadowed sheet standing on the field while done and blocked steps lie flat in it, with a heavy numeral glyph and one thick rule running the length of the run — it refuses white pages, serifs, hairline grids, a second bright hue, centred layout and any body copy longer than two lines.
