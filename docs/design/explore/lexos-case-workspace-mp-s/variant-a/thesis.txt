VARIANT A — command center (dense, keyboard-first)
=================================================

THESIS
This product wins because the user can hold the whole case — every dated fact and every open
obligation — in one viewport and act on any of it from the keyboard, without opening a section,
aiming at a button, or leaving the case to find the next one that needs him.

ART DIRECTION
Its art direction is a dark instrument: a graphite field with warm bone type, exactly one meaning
hue (signal amber, spent only on what is overdue or not yet recorded) plus a rust reserved for
destructive verbs, squarish wide-tracked caps for labels against a true monospace for every date,
amount, count and case number, rows dense enough to hold the entire case in one viewport, and a
flat hairline-ruled surface whose only ornaments are the inverted cursor row and a permanent
key-hint bar — it refuses white grounds, cards, shadows, icon sets, and any second bright hue.


WHAT THE PAGE IS BUILT OUT OF — the structural contract for A
-------------------------------------------------------------

1. PRIMARY OBJECT — the addressable row.
   Every hearing, document, task and note is a peer row in one of four co-present ledgers, and
   the caseload is a fifth column of case rows. The case is read as a full board of rows, and the
   cursor sits on exactly one of them at all times.

2. PRIMARY ACTION — fire a verb on the row under the cursor, from the keyboard.
   `record` / `file` / `add` / `edit` / `remove` are bound to keys and shown in a permanent hint
   bar at the foot; the matching inline verbs render on the CURSOR ROW ONLY, so exactly one row
   out of a page full of rows is actionable at a time. There is no global primary button anywhere
   on this page, and no actions panel.

3. INFO BEFORE ACTION — everything.
   The always-visible quartet as a dense top status line, plus every ledger's live count, plus
   each queued case's own overdue count and next hearing — all present before the first
   keystroke. A's whole claim is that nothing needs opening, so nothing is.

4. NAVIGATION MODEL — cursor and key; no destinations.
   Move within a ledger, across the four ledgers, and to the next case in the caseload column,
   all by key. No tabs, no steps, no drill-in, and no view ever replaces another. The caseload
   column is CONTENT — case rows carrying their own counts — never a rail of buttons.

5. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE — by depth inside a ledger, never by type.
   Every type is on screen at all times. Only depth is truncated, and truncation is numbered and
   expands in place ("+3 earlier documents"). Nothing is ever behind a control that names a
   category.

6. EXPERT PATH — keys over rows and over cases.
   Every verb has a key; the hint bar is permanent, not a tooltip; one key moves to the next case
   with a non-zero overdue count. Expertise here is never touching the mouse and never leaving
   the console.

7. FAILURE / RECOVERY — the write fails in the row.
   The row holds its position and gains a state cell with what failed and what to do next. The
   ledger counts do not move, because the counts are the truth about the record. No toast, no
   dialog, no modal. Return puts the cursor on the first overdue row.

DEBT 1 (the shipped content column is frozen at 416px from 1024px upward) — A's answer:
   ABOLISH THE MEASURE. There is no 48rem shell. The console fills the viewport to a wide cap and
   spends every pixel on co-presence: four ledgers of this case plus the caseload column, all
   live at once. Width buys more of the case on screen, never longer lines.

DEBT 2 (the shipped 16rem actions panel offers all four actions on every tab) — A's answer:
   NO PANEL; THE CURSOR IS THE HIERARCHY. Verbs are keys plus inline verbs on the cursor row.
   One row actionable at a time; no global action.

MOBILE (required):
   Ledgers stack in a fixed priority order — what the case owes first. The caseload column
   becomes a one-line strip of case rows with counts at the top. The cursor model degrades to
   select-a-row-then-its-inline-verbs. Density stays: a phone-sized A that turns airy has lost
   the thesis.


ART DIRECTION IN FULL — the four axes, all four are contract
------------------------------------------------------------

PALETTE — a graphite field, and never white.
   Ground deep graphite (~#191c20), one step up for rails (~#22262b), type in warm bone
   (~#e9e5dd) with a dimmer bone for meta. EXACTLY ONE MEANING HUE: signal amber (~#e8a33d),
   spent only on what is overdue or not yet recorded — nothing that is fine is ever coloured. One
   reserved hue, rust (~#e0705c), used nowhere but destructive verbs. Hexes are direction, not
   specification: hit the hue and the temperature, tune the value until it passes contrast.
   REFUSES: white or paper grounds, a second bright hue, colour on anything healthy, glow, neon,
   gradients as decoration.

TYPOGRAPHY — a console voice: caps labels against a true monospace.
   Column heads and labels small, wide-tracked, all-caps, in a squarish grotesk. EVERY date,
   amount, count and case number in a genuine monospace so the columns lock down the page. FOUR
   tight steps plus ONE violent exception: the overdue count is set enormous and nothing else
   comes near it. The first family in each stack must be genuinely available with no network
   (e.g. Consolas / Lucida Console for data; Tahoma / Franklin Gothic Medium / Arial for labels)
   — a webfont that fails to fetch destroys this direction.
   REFUSES: serifs, mixed-case labels, anything above 15px in body rows, decorative weight
   changes.

DENSITY & RHYTHM — maximum density, read across.
   Roughly 28–32px rows on a strict grid, columns locked by the monospace, the whole case in one
   viewport at 1280. Air exists only in the gutters between ledgers. The eye is paced by the
   cursor, which is the only moving thing on the page.

SURFACE & ORNAMENT — flat, ruled, lit only by inversion.
   Zero shadow. Hairline rules in a lighter graphite. Radius <=2px. Two real ornaments: the
   CURSOR ROW INVERTS (bone ground, graphite type) so the eye finds it instantly, and a PERMANENT
   KEY-HINT BAR at the foot rendering the closed verb set against its keys. A 2px state tick in a
   row's left gutter, always beside a word.
   REFUSES: cards, shadows, rounded surfaces, illustration, icon sets.

WHAT THIS VARIANT MUST NOT BECOME
   This thesis is many-things-at-once, under a cursor. If the page ever shows one thing at a time
   — or merges the four ledgers into a single dated column with one compose box at its head, or
   sprouts an ordered run of steps with a next button, or hides a type of item behind a control
   that names that type — it has stopped being this thesis, however good it looks.

CANONICAL COPY FOR THE STATES — use these words, do not write your own
  loading      The case header and your page's primary region each need a designed loading state.
               No copy: quiet placeholder shapes in your own visual system. A region that shows
               nothing while it waits is not a loading state, and the shipped build's absence of
               one is a named defect this explore must answer.
  empty        "Next hearing — Not set" on this case, and Latha Bhaskaran's empty case-number
               field, are the real empty states. An empty field still shows: in a legal record an
               empty field is information.
  error        "Not saved — the connection dropped. What you typed is kept here. Save again."
  success      The recorded fact appears in place, dated — "Outcome recorded · 14 Jul 2026" — and
               what is due next recomputes in the same view. Recording an outcome does NOT change
               the overdue count: it stays 2, because neither the task nor the filing was
               touched. Do not invent different arithmetic.
  disabled     A blocked affordance states its reason: "Cannot add the next hearing until the
               14 Jul 2026 outcome is recorded."
  destructive  "Remove the document Affidavit of the plaintiff? A removed document cannot be
               brought back." A destructive verb is never styled like the benign verb beside it.
  end state    Only if your thesis renders a finished screen, verbatim: "Nothing due on this case
               today. Next: Vishnu Menon v. Ashirvad Developers · hearing 04 Aug 2026."

--- SHARED FLOOR — IDENTICAL IN A, B AND C. Not a divergence surface. ---

A variant that "innovates" on anything in this block has diverged on the wrong axis.

- ALWAYS VISIBLE, at all times, in every variant: parties · case number (where one exists) ·
  status · next hearing · overdue count. Never behind a click, a period, a step, a keystroke or a
  scroll.
- A FIELD THAT EXISTS AND IS EMPTY STILL SHOWS. "Next hearing — Not set" is information in a
  legal record. Never hidden, never collapsed, never a bare dash.
- ALL FIVE STATES ARE REAL STATES — empty · loading · error · success · disabled — rendered where
  they naturally occur. Keep the state matrix OFF the product page. If you need a reference
  block, put it at the foot, mark it as reference, and keep it visually subordinate. A previous
  cycle let state galleries eat 40–60% of every variant's page; that alone reads as BELOW-BAR.
- CLOSED VOCABULARY. Nouns: case · hearing · document · task · note · client · firm · case number
  · court · case type · filing. Verbs: add · edit · remove · save · record · file. `set`,
  `schedule`, `reschedule`, `close`, `reopen`, `approve`, `complete` are NOT verbs in this
  product — "set the next hearing" renders as "Add hearing". "Hearing outcome" is sanctioned.
  A document's own TITLE is content a lawyer typed and may be real court vocabulary; an interface
  LABEL — button, column head, step name, status, column of a table — may use only the closed set.
- STATUSES ARE EXACTLY INTAKE · ACTIVE · ON_HOLD · CLOSED, rendered as human text ("On hold",
  never ON_HOLD), and never carried by colour alone. No fifth status, and no status-shaped word
  invented for a step, a period, a row state or a queue.
- VOICE: a court record, not an assistant. State the fact and its date. No exclamation marks, no
  encouragement, no congratulation for filing something. Anti-words: urgent · flimsy · chatty.
  Feel words: exact · unhurried · durable.
- DESTRUCTIVE LANGUAGE names the thing and states that it cannot be undone; errors say what
  failed and what to do next.
- DENSITY RULES: one row per hearing, per document, per task · dates and amounts in tabular
  numerals so they align down a column · a row's meta separated by " · " consistently.
- DATES as 27 Jul 2026. ₹ AMOUNTS in Indian digit grouping (₹18,45,000). Lorem ipsum is an
  automatic VIOLATION anywhere, including inside a collapsed period, a truncated ledger or a
  disabled step.
- NEITHER SHIPPED SHAPE SURVIVES: no variant keeps a vertical 16rem rail of buttons, and no
  variant keeps a five-item section tab rail. A page with either has re-skinned the baseline
  instead of answering the two debts this explore exists to answer.
- A11Y FLOOR: >=4.5:1 on every text pair you rely on, measured against YOUR OWN declared values
  and commented in tokens.css · targets >=44px · visible focus · reduced motion honoured. Colour
  is never the only carrier of a state: every coloured thing also carries a word or a mark.
- DESKTOP AND MOBILE are both required; tablet is declared `no`. State your reflow in the page —
  a layout that only works at 1280px is not a thesis.
