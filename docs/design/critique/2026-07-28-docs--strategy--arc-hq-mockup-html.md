## Findings

- WEAKNESS: timeline category-dot colours are reused across unrelated categories with no legend, and the same underlying entity is colour-coded inconsistently across panels — the 14:47 "arc-oss" row in the Today timeline uses a green dot, while the "arc (open-source)" card in the Portfolio panel (same product) uses a purple dot — Today timeline (left column) vs. Portfolio (bottom of left column) — with zero legend anywhere on the page, colour currently carries no reliable meaning and two independent colour systems risk being read as one.
- WEAKNESS: "YOUR TIME NEEDED" (9 min, 3 decisions in inbox) and the Approval Inbox panel that contains those decisions carry no more visual priority than the purely informational KPI tiles and read-only panels beside them — top KPI row (6th tile) and the right column's "APPROVAL INBOX" header — same card style, weight, and colour as everything else, despite the banner directly above stating this is the one thing that needs the viewer's time.
- WEAKNESS: the left and right columns end at very different heights, leaving a large empty void at the bottom-right of the page — right column stops after "WHAT THE COMPANY LEARNED THIS WEEK" while the left column continues roughly 200-250px further through the Portfolio cards — only visible now that the full page is captured; the page reads bottom-heavy/lopsided rather than balanced.
- WEAKNESS: the small "▤ run / ▤ verdict / ▤ diff / ▤ link / ▤ bundle / ▤ thread / ▤ txn / ▤ auto / ▤ report / ▤ log / ▤ delta" evidence-link glyphs repeated on all 11 Today-timeline rows render at roughly half the height of the now-fixed 44px buttons — despite the panel's own caption instructing "click ▤ for evidence," i.e. these are documented, repeated interactive targets, and they read as sub-floor on the a11y target-size check. (Judged by visual comparison against the confirmed-44px buttons, not a pixel-exact measurement — no pixel-measurement tool is available in this critique pass.)
- POLISH: the Portfolio panel's 3-column grid holds 5 cards, leaving the third cell of row 2 (after "PromptVault") blank — bottom of the left column — reads as a minor count mismatch rather than a designed empty state.
- POLISH: the footer disclaimer ("ARC HQ · concept mock v0 · every number here is illustrative…") renders noticeably fainter than the rest of the muted text on the page — bottom of page, centred — probably intentional de-emphasis for a footnote, worth a contrast check only if this line ever needs to carry required information.

## What is working
Both of round 2's confirmed fixes hold up under the fuller page: buttons are consistently sized with no regressions in card/row rhythm, and muted text is legible everywhere at normal reading sizes. The previously-filed Autonomy Ladder "zero content" violation is now visibly a render-recipe gap rather than a real defect — worth remembering when judging future viewport-only captures. Beyond the fixes: the type scale is small and disciplined (KPI numbers, body, small-caps labels, tiny badges — each size tied to a real hierarchy role, not scattered), there's no lorem ipsum, no gradient-hero, no emoji-as-icon, currency formatting is correct for the locale (₹1,54,300 lakh-style), and corner-radius/card treatment stays consistent across KPI tiles, panels, and pill badges throughout the now-visible full page.
